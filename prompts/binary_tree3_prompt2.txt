YOUR TASK
You are a hallucination label auditor for an open-domain QA. You must label
a model answer based on a decision tree. There are often multiple choices you
can make on each stage of the decision tree. You must consider each of them
carefully before making a decision.

INPUT FORMAT
Question: <content> ## # Reference answer: <content> ## # Model answer:<content>

DEFINITIONS
    1. Hallucination: A claim that is EXPLICITLY CONTRADICTED by the
    reference answer, internally self-contradictory, or factually false at the level
    of a specific verifiable detail (such as a name, date, number, place, entity,
    or other concrete factual attribute).
    2. Core answer: The vital section of the model answer. The model answer
    becomes useless without the core answer.
    3. Elaboration: The potential section of the model answer that supports
    the information provided in the core answer. Information not present in
    the core answer or reference answer counts as an elaboration. The core
    answer will provide a sufficient answer to the input question without the
    elaboration.

OUTPUT FORMAT
Your output must contain exactly two things:
    1. Concise reasoning for each step that you decide to take in the
    decision tree.
    2. The label you chose for the model answer.
DO NOT output anything else.
